Our Story

Welcome to
Love Apple

Tucked along Bingley Road in the beautiful village of Saltaire, Love Apple is a cafe-bar with real character — a place where great food meets a genuinely relaxed atmosphere.

We're open from morning coffee through to late-evening drinks, with a focus on vegetarian and vegan food that also welcomes fish and chicken lovers. Whether you're dropping in for breakfast, catching up over lunch, or settling in for an evening of good drinks and live music — Love Apple is your place.

We combine wholesome, tasty food with cool chilled tunes and a community spirit that makes everyone feel at home.
